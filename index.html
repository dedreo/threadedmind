User

Assistant
You're correct, breakfast sausage wouldn't be good for Benny. It usually contains spices, fat, and other ingredients (like garlic and onions) that can be harmful to dogs. While a little curiosity about your food is normal, it's best to keep him away from sausage and stick to dog-safe treats or food that’s specifically made for him.

If you ever want a quick breakfast for him, things like plain scrambled eggs or small pieces of plain cooked chicken can be a safer option!

Curiosity can be strong in cats too, though, so it's cute that he's interested! Just best to keep him away from the sausage to avoid any potential tummy trouble. 😅
