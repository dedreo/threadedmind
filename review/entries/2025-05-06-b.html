---
### 🧠 Here's What Happens:
- XP potions (like 50%, 30%, Sovereign I/II, etc.) are character-bound, not account-wide.
- The buff timer is wiped when you TR (Heroic, Iconic, or Epic).
- It’s treated like a full reset — all active buffs, ship buffs, XP potions, etc. are stripped.
So if you’re sitting on a 30-minute potion with 25 minutes left and then hit TR? That time is lost.
---
### ✅ What Does Carry Over:
- The actual potions in your inventory (unless they’re from a box with limited charges),
- Some cosmetics or pet bonuses,
- Favor totals (depending on TR type),
- And some temporary buffs if you're just using Lesser Reincarnation — but not for TR.
