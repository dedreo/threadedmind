Absolutely—air fryer to the rescue. Here's your quick-and-dirty method for refrigerated (pre-breaded) okra:
---
### 🔥 Air Fryer Method (Breaded Okra)
1. Preheat your air fryer to 400°F (204°C) – about 3–5 minutes.
2. Lightly spray the basket (or toss okra in a tiny bit of oil) to prevent sticking and help crisping.
3. Place okra in a single layer.
Don’t overcrowd—air needs to circulate.
4. Cook for 10–12 minutes, shaking or flipping halfway through at around the 6-minute mark.
5. Check for crispiness.
If still a little soft, go another 2–3 minutes until golden and crisp.
---
### ✅ Optional Power Moves:
- Dust with a little Cajun seasoning or smoked paprika after cooking.
- Serve with ranch, hot sauce, or a spitefully small cup of honey mustard.
Way easier than deep-frying, and zero vat of oil required. You’ll still get the crunch—without needing a post-snack shower.
