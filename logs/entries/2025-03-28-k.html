Assistant
Yes, some cats can be trained to use a human toilet, though it's not an easy or natural process for them. There are toilet training kits available that help guide the cat through the process. These kits usually start with a small training seat placed on the toilet, and over time, the cat becomes accustomed to using the toilet. The goal is to slowly transition from a litter box to the toilet, but it can take a lot of patience and consistency.

However, it’s not ideal for all cats. Some might never get the hang of it, and others may feel stressed by the change. Plus, it's important to remember that once a cat is toilet-trained, they no longer have access to a litter box, so their bathroom habits might be affected if something goes wrong. It’s definitely a bit of a niche trick for cat owners, but some people swear by it!
