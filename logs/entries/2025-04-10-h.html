### 🧠 **The Moral Machine** (by MIT)
It’s an online ethics experiment that throws self-driving car versions of the trolley problem at you. Stuff like:
- Do you hit the jaywalking criminals or the law-abiding senior citizens?
- Save the dog or the pregnant woman?

Then it shows you your "moral profile" after a few dozen choices—like how much you prioritize youth, lawfulness, or saving more lives. Kinda chilling, kinda fascinating.

### 🃏 **Trial by Trolley** (tabletop game)
Made by the *Cyanide & Happiness* crew. You play as the trolley conductor and have to choose who to run over, while other players try to stack their side with “good” characters (like puppies or your grandma) and sabotage the other side with “bad” ones (like “a billionaire who just poached a baby seal”).

It turns into hilarious chaos, and if you’re the type to enjoy twisted logic debates with friends, it’s pure gold.
