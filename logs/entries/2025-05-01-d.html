Game Title: Icepick

Synopsis: Icepick is a unique twist on the classic Tetris formula, where icy-themed blocks and whimsical blobs collide in a fast-paced puzzle game. The game features traditional Tetris mechanics with an added layer of strategy through the introduction of "blobs," which appear randomly on the screen. These blobs can be captured and frozen within the falling ice blocks, adding a new dynamic to the gameplay.

Core Gameplay:

Players drop Tetriminoes onto the grid, aiming to complete lines and clear the board.

The key twist: Players can capture and freeze blobs inside the blocks. When a line is completed with frozen blobs, special power-ups are activated.

Power-ups vary based on blob colors, such as:

Snowflake: Alters the next falling piece to a more challenging or unique shape.

Line Blast: Adds lines to an opponent’s board.

Blob Snipe: Removes a trapped blob from the opponent’s board, potentially ruining a line in the process.

Modes:

Single-player: Classic Tetris with the blob mechanics and AI opponents.

Multiplayer: Split-screen couch co-op or versus mode, with power-ups affecting both players' grids.

Online Play: (Future development) Players can compete against each other in real-time.

Controls:

Players can use arrow keys or custom controls for piece movement, with additional keys for power-ups.

The "down" button speeds up the descent of the blocks, but does not drop them immediately—allowing players to "scoop" blobs into place.

Visual and Sound:

The game is set in an icy world with snowflake-like blocks and blobs that move slowly across the screen in semi-random patterns.

Fun sound effects for freezing blobs, clearing lines, and activating power-ups, alongside a light-hearted background soundtrack to maintain a fun, casual atmosphere.
