User
so explain e.g. is that basically "in example"?

Assistant
Close! **"e.g."** is short for the Latin phrase *exempli gratia*, which means **"for example."**

So when you see **e.g.**, it introduces **one or more examples** of something, but not an exhaustive list.

For comparison:
- **e.g.** → "for example"
- *You should eat more protein-rich foods (e.g., chicken, fish, tofu).*
- **i.e.** → "that is" / "in other words"
- *You should eat more protein-rich foods (i.e., foods that help build muscle and keep you full).*

User
Oh, one other thing I wonder: in AA and such they make a big deal over the idea of dying and not being sober. How often in general and on average would any given individual die suddenly, so it wouldn't even matter?

Assistant
That’s an interesting and kind of existential question. If we’re talking about **sudden, unexpected deaths**, those are actually a smaller percentage of overall deaths.

### **How Often Do People Die Suddenly?**
Sudden deaths are typically caused by things like:
- **Heart attacks & strokes** (biggest cause)
- **Accidents (car crashes, falls, etc.)**
- **Violence (homicides, suicides, overdoses)**
- **Sudden cardiac arrest** (especially in older individuals or those with health conditions)

#### **Some rough statistics (U.S.):**
- **About 10-15% of all deaths** are sudden and unexpected.
- **Sudden cardiac arrest alone** kills about **350,000 people per year** (~0.1% of the U.S. population annually).
- **Accidents (unintentional injuries)** cause around **6% of total deaths** annually.

### **So, What Are the Odds?**
For any random person, the chance of dying suddenly **in a given year** is **well under 1%**—maybe **0.1% to 0.3%** depending on age and health. That means **over 99% of people will have some warning** (even if it’s just hours or days).

### **Does This Undermine the "Dying Sober" Idea?**
It depends on perspective. If you're in recovery, you probably assume you'll have time to reflect on your sobriety toward the end. But technically, most people **won't** die instantly, so **how you live daily still matters**. AA’s philosophy is more about personal integrity—whether or not you’d regret drinking if you **did** suddenly die.

### **Final Thought:**
If you’re thinking purely in numbers, **the chances of suddenly dying drunk vs. sober are small**. But if someone drinks regularly, the bigger risk isn’t dying suddenly—it’s health decline, poor decisions, or relapse leading to worse outcomes over time.
