Cats are smart like that; they learn patterns and pick up on little cues, probably recognizing the sound of your car or footsteps, too. Definitely a sign that he’s bonded with you! 😊

User
do cats like cantaloup?

Assistant
Yep! Some cats actually *love* cantaloupe and other melons. It’s thought that the fruit’s aroma mimics certain amino acids found in meat, which makes it oddly appealing to them. It’s safe in small amounts as an occasional treat, as long as there are no seeds or rinds involved.

That said, not all cats are into it—Benny might sniff it and walk away, or he might go nuts for it. Worth a tiny experiment! 😸

Nice! Sounds like he’s at least *curious* about it. Some cats just like licking the juice off instead of actually eating it. If he keeps at it, maybe he’ll develop a taste for it. Either way, it's fun seeing what he does with new things! 😆
