- Abutments
- Crowns / implant-supported denture
- Final prosthetics

→ Burn **Year 2 annual max**

That’s **$6k-ish insurer money** for work that *medically* belongs together.

## 2️⃣ Can you carry *two* dental plans at once?
Short answer: **yes**, but with caveats.

### This is called **coordination of benefits (COB)**

You can:

⚠️ **They do NOT stack limits.**
You do *not* get:

Instead:
- One is **primary**
- One is **secondary**
- Secondary only pays what the primary didn’t — *up to what it would’ve paid anyway*

So if:
- Primary pays 50%
- Secondary also covers 50%

You *might* approach 80–100% **of the allowed amount**, but:
- still capped by each plan’s annual max
- still limited by “allowable charges”
