Assistant
Totally fair curiosity—and honestly, that's exactly the kind of thing I’d wonder, too.

If you *do* try giving him a small portion tomorrow, here are a few ways to play it safe:

1. **Give a very small amount**, like a teaspoon or two. Enough to taste, but not enough to overload his stomach.
2. **Mix it with something familiar**, like a little dry Meow Mix, to buffer it.
3. **Watch him closely for a few hours afterward**—no distractions this time. If you notice any signs of queasiness or distress (licking lips a lot, hiding, stomach gurgles, etc.), pull the plug on that food permanently.
4. **Double-check the packaging and expiration date**, just in case something was off and you didn’t notice it in the moment.

It’s possible he just scarfed it down too fast—especially since you mentioned he went in for seconds. Some cats can’t handle sudden rich meals if they’re used to more basic kibble, kind of like us eating three gas station burritos after a fast. 😬
