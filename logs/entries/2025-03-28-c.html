Assistant
Yeah, that’s a solid way to keep things looking **intentional** while still growing it out. Use a trimmer with a **longer guard setting** (like a #4 or #5) to clean up around the ears and sideburns so it doesn’t start looking **puffy or uneven**.

Some general tips:
- **Around the ears & sideburns** → Use a **long guard (or scissors)** to blend the sides, so it stays neat but doesn’t ruin the growth.
- **Neckline & stray hairs** → Trim the back of your neck **every couple of weeks** to keep things sharp.
- **Top & back** → Leave it alone for now if you want length, but you can **lightly shape it** so it grows evenly.
- **Styling product (optional)** → If curls are getting **too unruly**, a **light leave-in conditioner** or curl cream can help keep things **defined instead of frizzy**.

Basically, you’re just **maintaining the shape** without cutting too much. The sides and back tend to grow out faster and look wild before the top catches up, so just keeping those areas **blended and tidy** will make the in-between phase look **intentional instead of awkward**.
