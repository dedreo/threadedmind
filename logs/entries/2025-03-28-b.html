# Foresight

Assistant
It's definitely not a mainstream concept, but it's not entirely unheard of either. Some people have shared AI-generated conversations on Reddit, personal blogs, or Twitter/X, but a dedicated site curating *interesting* or *insightful* exchanges would be a unique take—especially if you add commentary or context about why certain conversations stood out to you.

If you mix it with other content, like personal thoughts, media analysis, or even reflections on AI interactions, it could have a niche appeal. Would you want it to be more of an archival project, a casual blog, or something interactive where others could submit/chat too?

Are you thinking of structuring it like a blog with individual posts, or more like a categorized archive where people can browse by topic?
