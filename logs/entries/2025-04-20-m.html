Buckles can be tricky, especially when it comes to harnesses! You want to make sure it’s snug enough to stay on securely but not so tight that it’s uncomfortable for Benny.

Here’s a general guide on how to adjust it:

1. **Straps before the buckle**: When you put the harness on, adjust the straps before you buckle them to ensure they are loose enough to fit comfortably over his body, but not so loose that they can slide off easily.

2. **Tighten the harness**: After the buckle is fastened, you should pull the loose end of the strap to tighten the harness. The goal is to have it snug enough that it won't easily slip off, but you should still be able to fit two fingers between the harness and Benny’s body (especially around his neck and chest area).

3. **Post-buckle slack**: If you’re talking about slack after the buckle, you want to make sure you’re pulling the strap *away* from the buckle to tighten it properly. You’ll want to make sure the harness is comfortably tight without being restrictive.

It sounds like you might be trying to figure out if you need to add slack after the buckle to get the right fit. Usually, it’s the opposite: tightening the strap after buckling (with a little slack left over) helps the harness stay snug and secure.

The fact that Benny’s handling it well in the harness shows you’re getting it right, even if it’s still a bit of a trial and error process.
