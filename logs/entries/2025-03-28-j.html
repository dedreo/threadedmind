Totally fair—coconut is one of *those* flavors that can dominate if it shows up uninvited. In classic Dole Whip, the coconut flavor *can* be present if it’s made with coconut milk, but it’s not required at all. The original Disneyland version is actually dairy-free and uses a kind of soft-serve base, but when making it at home, people often sub in regular milk or almond/oat milk instead. You can absolutely use regular milk or even vanilla almond milk if you want it creamy without coconut notes.

So yep—if you're not into coconut, skip it! Go for:

- Frozen pineapple chunks (1 heaping cup)
- 1/4 cup milk (your pick)
- Optional: splash of vanilla extract or a bit of honey/maple syrup if you like it sweeter

Blend it up until it’s smooth and soft-serve-like. That’s it! Want me to write up a cute little index-card-style version for the fridge or note app?

### A few close-ish alternatives or homemade options:
- **Pipe cleaners** (aka chenille stems): You can twist a few into tight coils or springs. Not quite as bouncy but the texture is similar and cats usually go wild for them.
- **Hair ties** (fabric-covered, not the rubber ones): Some cats love batting them around and carrying them.
- **Cardboard spiral**: Take a thin strip of cardboard and twist it into a curl—kinda hit or miss but some cats love the “crinkle”.
- **Online bulk packs**: Amazon or Chewy sometimes have off-brand wire spring toys in packs of 10–20 for cheap. You could even check Temu or AliExpress if you're okay waiting a bit longer for shipping.
