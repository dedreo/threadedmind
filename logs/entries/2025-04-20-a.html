---

### 🍖 Wet Food Types 101:
Here's a quick breakdown of common textures:

- **Pâté** (you got it right, pronounced *pa-tay*):
A smooth, dense paste. No chunks, just a meaty loaf texture. Some cats love it, others lick the top and walk off.

- **Chunks in Gravy / Stew**:
Meat or fish bits in a broth or sauce. Often the kind cats lick all the gravy off of and leave the chunks behind. 😹

- **Shreds / Flaked / Minced**:
Finely shredded meat with light gravy. Easier to eat for some picky cats.

- **Mousse / Soufflé / Creamy**:
Lighter than pâté. Some brands do this style for older cats or sensitive teeth.

---

### 💧 Benny’s Move = Classic Cat Behavior
If he “drained all the wet” and left the solids, that usually means:
- It’s a **chunk-in-gravy** type, and he's a *gravy-guy* (super common).
- Or even if it’s a pâté, he may just like the surface layer and moisture the most.

So yeah, **adding water is a smart move**! 💦
It rehydrates the food *and* gives him more of that juicy texture he clearly prefers. Cats are notorious for low thirst drives, so any extra moisture is a win.

---

### 🎯 What You Can Try in the Future:
- **Mix the food well before serving** so he doesn't just lap the gravy off the top.
- **Warm it up slightly** after fridging (like 5 seconds in the microwave—test the temp with your finger first!).
- Add **a spoon of warm water or unsalted broth** to revive the aroma and texture.
