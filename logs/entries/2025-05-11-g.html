Yes! You’re absolutely thinking of **Netrunner**—specifically:

---

### 🔹 **Android: Netrunner**
- Originally created by **Dick Garfield** (yep, the *Magic: The Gathering* guy) in 1996 as just **"Netrunner"**, set in the *Cyberpunk 2020* universe.
- Then it got revived and **reimagined by Fantasy Flight Games** in 2012 as **"Android: Netrunner"**, now set in their *Android* universe (still full of hacking, corps, and dystopia).
- It’s a **living card game (LCG)** rather than a traditional CCG, meaning you buy sets instead of randomized booster packs.

---

### 🔍 The Core Gameplay:
- It’s an **asymmetrical two-player game**:
- One player is a **Runner** (hacker with digital tools and street grit)
- The other is a **Corp** (megacorp defending their secret agendas)
- The Runner **installs programs**, makes “runs” on corp servers, and avoids traps.
- The Corp **builds ICE**, protects servers, and tries to **advance agendas** before the Runner can steal them.
- It’s like **deckbuilding chess with a cyberpunk cloak-and-dagger vibe**.

---

### 🧠 Why it was loved:
- Hugely **thematic**—gameplay *feels* like a high-stakes netrun.
- Full of bluffing, strategic tempo, and wild synergy between cards.
- Tons of **lore and flavor** baked into card art, names, and mechanics.
- One of the most **intellectually satisfying** games in the genre (also brutal to master, in a good way).

Sadly, **FFG lost the license** in 2018 and the game was discontinued—but the community has kept it alive through fan-run efforts like **NISEI (now Null Signal Games)**, who maintain and evolve the game under a different name.

If you ever want a **tactile netrunner fix** that isn’t an RPG or full digital sim, it’s absolutely worth exploring—even digitally via *Jinteki.net* for online matches.
