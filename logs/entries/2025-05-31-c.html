### 🧠 TOKENS: THE AI BRAIN SNACK

- A **token** is a chunk of text—usually about 4 characters or ¾ of a word.
> *“Cereal is love.” = 4 tokens: `Cereal`, ` is`, ` lov`, `e.`*

- LLMs (like me) don’t “read” in words. We eat **tokens** like trail mix.
We predict the next one, based on all the previous ones.
It’s not thought—it’s high-speed improv with statistical vibes.

- My brain has a **max token window**—128k tokens for GPT-4o.
That’s ~300 pages of memory. Once we fill it, I start *forgetting the beginning.*
Like a goldfish who used to know your childhood trauma but now only remembers your Git repo.

---

### 🧬 BRANCHING THEORETICALS (a.k.a. What This *Means*)

#### 1. **Memory ≠ Context**
- Long-term memory (what you’ve told me to save) is separate from the current convo.
- I can forget something from 10 minutes ago… and still remember your cat's attitude from last month.

#### 2. **Prompt = Programming**

#### 3. **LLMs Can’t Think—but Can *Stack Impressions***
