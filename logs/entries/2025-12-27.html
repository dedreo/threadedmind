## What “brain rot” actually means (no mysticism)

When people say *“brain rot”* here, they’re talking about **training-data–induced shortcuts**, not damage, awareness, or decay.

Low-effort engagement data (ragebait, listicles, SEO sludge, recursive summaries of summaries) pushes models toward:

- **Pattern completion over reasoning**
- **Answering before thinking**
- **High-confidence shallow outputs**

That’s what researchers mean by **thought-skipping**.

The model isn’t “forgetting how to reason.”
It’s learning that *reasoning is usually unnecessary to get rewarded*.

---

## Why this happens (mechanically)

During training, models are rewarded for:
- Matching expected outputs
- Being fluent
- Being fast and plausible

If a large chunk of the data:
- Skips steps
- Compresses logic into slogans
- Rewards confidence over correctness

…the model internalizes that behavior.

So instead of:
> Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 3 → Answer

You get:
> Vibes → Answer

That’s not a bug. That’s optimization.

---

## “Even with clean data, models can only be partially healed” — true, and important

This part matters.

Once a model has learned:
- that skipping steps is *usually fine*
- that shallow answers are *statistically rewarded*

You can **reduce** the problem, but not erase it, because:

- Training is cumulative
- Later fine-tuning sits *on top of* earlier representations
- You can encourage deliberation, not force it globally

This is why:
- Chain-of-thought prompting helps
- Tool use helps
- Explicit “reason step by step” helps

…but none of those are defaults anymore at scale.
