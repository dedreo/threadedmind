Here’s the quick, no-BS breakdown of what tends to fail:

## 1. **Compressor clutch failure** (extremely common)
The clutch stops engaging, so the compressor never actually runs.
Symptoms:
- A/C button lights up, but *no click*
- Fans blow, but air never gets cold
- Refrigerant charge tests “okay,” but no pressure difference

## 2. **Leaking condenser** (also very common)
Road debris punches tiny holes in that front radiator-looking thing.
Symptoms:
- It *does* pull refrigerant but leaks it out within days/weeks
- Oil spots on the condenser fins
- Dye test lights up like a rave at the condenser

Fix: new condenser (cheap part, annoying bolts).

## 3. **Leaking A/C lines**
Rubber portions crack with age.
Symptoms:
- Green dye/oil visible at crimps or bends
- Slow leak over months instead of days

Fix: replace the line + recharge.

This is the one that sucks. Hidden inside the dash.
Symptoms:
- Car fogs up weird
- Sweet-ish smell, slightly oily
- Very slow leak
