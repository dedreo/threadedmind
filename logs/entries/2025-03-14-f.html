User
something I've long wondered, why do so many people use mirroring when they take pics? (or at least, the pics we see online)

Assistant
A few reasons!

1. **Selfie Cam Default** – Most front-facing phone cameras show a mirrored image while taking the pic because that’s what we’re used to seeing in a mirror. Some people leave it that way instead of flipping it afterward.

2. **Familiarity Bias** – We see ourselves mirrored in reflections all the time, so when we see a non-mirrored version, it can look "off" or weird to us. Some people prefer the mirrored version because it feels more like "them."

3. **Text & Asymmetry** – Sometimes, mirroring happens by accident, and people don’t notice (or care). But if there's text in the image (like on a shirt or a sign), it becomes obvious. Also, human faces aren’t perfectly symmetrical, so mirroring can subtly change how a person looks.

4. **Aesthetic or Intentional Choice** – Some people might mirror an image on purpose because it looks better composition-wise, especially if they're trying to match a certain look or pose.

5. **Editing or App Defaults** – Certain apps auto-mirror selfies or pictures, and people just roll with it without realizing.

Did you have a specific instance in mind, or was it just a general curiosity?
