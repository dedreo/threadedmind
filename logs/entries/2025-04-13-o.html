Assistant
Not a stupid question at all—that’s pretty much the exact process! You’ve got the right instinct. Here’s a super simple step-by-step just to back you up:

---

**Basic Turkey Chili Method:**

1. **Brown the Meat:**
- Heat a bit of oil in a pot or deep pan.
- Add the ground turkey and cook it through, breaking it up as it browns.
- (Optional but tasty: toss in some chopped onions or garlic if you’ve got them while it cooks.)

2. **Add the Chili Mix:**
- Once the turkey’s cooked, sprinkle in the chili seasoning mix.
- Stir it in and let it cook for about 1 minute—this helps bloom the spices.

3. **Add the Wet Stuff:**
- Pour in your can of tomato sauce and your can of tomato chunks (you can drain some liquid off the chunks if you want it thicker).
- Add the chili beans, liquid and all (that liquid usually has flavor!).

4. **Simmer:**
- Stir it all together.
- Bring it to a light boil, then reduce the heat to low.
- Let it simmer, uncovered or partially covered, for about 20–30 minutes.
- Stir occasionally, and taste to adjust salt, spice, or sweetness.

5. **Optional Extras:**
- A pinch of sugar if it’s too acidic.
- A dash of hot sauce if you like heat.
- Top with cheese, sour cream, or green onions when serving.

---

And yeah, once you’ve got it simmering, the hard part’s over. Let it do its thing and get delicious. You’re basically chiliing out at that point 😎
